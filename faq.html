Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our fragrances, shipping, and more.

Ordering and Payment
How do I place an order on Grootman Fragrances?

Add your preferred fragrances to the cart, proceed to checkout, fill in your delivery details, and choose a payment method.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept bank deposit and immediate transfer. Use your order reference as the payment reference. EFT through the website is not available yet.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available in selected areas only. Contact us to confirm availability in your location.

Where do I get your bank details for payment?

Our bank details are provided during checkout and can also be requested via WhatsApp for verification before payment.

Delivery and Shipping
How long does delivery take?

Delivery normally takes 1 to 3 working days depending on your location.

What are the delivery fees?

Delivery fees vary by location. The exact amount will be shown during checkout or provided when you place your order.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Same-day delivery is available in selected areas. Contact us to confirm.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, we deliver anywhere in South Africa.

Returns and Refunds
What is your return policy?

Returns are accepted within 3 to 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unopened, and in their original packaging. Perfumes that have been sprayed, opened, or damaged cannot be returned due to hygiene reasons. Proof of purchase is required for all returns.

What is your return policy?

Returns are accepted within 3 to 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unopened, and in their original packaging. Perfumes that have been sprayed, opened, or damaged cannot be returned due to hygiene reasons. Proof of purchase is required for all returns.

Can I exchange an item?

Exchanges are only possible if the item is unopened and in original condition. Please contact us within 3 to 7 days of delivery to arrange an exchange.

Product Authenticity
Are your fragrances original?

Yes, all fragrances are authentic Dubai/Arab perfumes sourced directly from trusted suppliers.

Where do your perfumes come from?

Our collection comes from reputable fragrance manufacturers.

How long does the scent last?

Longevity varies per fragrance, but most last between 6 to 12 hours.

Customer Support
How do I contact support?

You can contact us via WhatsApp for fast responses, through TikTok messages, or by using the contact form on our website.

What are your operating hours?

We are available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

How long do responses take?

We respond within 24 hours.

Wholesale / Bulk Orders
Do you offer reseller prices?

No, we currently do not offer reseller or wholesale pricing.

Can I order in bulk for events or gifting?

Yes, bulk ordering for events, gifting, or special occasions is available on request.

General Store Info
Do you have a physical store?

Not yet. We currently operate online only.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

No, gift wrapping is not available at the moment.

Do you restock sold-out perfumes?

Yes, restocks happen regularly depending on supplier availability.

Which months do you operate?

We operate year-round from January to December.

Do you offer meetups or collection points?

Yes, meetups are available at Pretoria Central (Sammy Marks Square), Soshanguve (Crossing Mall), and Standerton (Monument Centre).
